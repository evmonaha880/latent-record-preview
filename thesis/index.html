The thesis

At 3:17 pm on 3 August Evan and Claude laid out ten thesis ideas. None won. At 7:38 pm the controlling idea arrived, and it was on none of the ten.

The ten that came first · 15:17

Several were given a job in the essay instead: the frame, the engine, the method, the opening, the moral stake.

The Molecule Was the Moat

Physical infrastructure buys a monopoly, then charges interest.

1What became of it The physical scale survives as one line: the promise on the box needed a continent behind it, and he built that too.
The essay it would have made

Kodak as the protagonist and Eastman as prologue: the emulsion as the moat, cattle-bone gelatin and silver by the ton, a satellite film capsule snatched from the sky in 1960, and the day in 1976 when the most demanding customer on earth stopped needing atoms.

The Man Who Could Not Keep Anything

A man who could not keep what he loved taught the world to keep everything.

2What became of it The mother and the ending stayed; the wounded-boy reading did not, because the biography had disproved the poverty it rests on.
The essay it would have made

A life told as loss: the father dead when he was seven, the mother's gloves rolled into two balls beside the bed, the machine for not losing things, and a company that could not let go of film.

Mr. Smith

The man who made the world visible engineered his own invisibility.

3What became of it Carried whole into the middle of the essay: the Hotel Belmont evening, the eight years of silence, the man inside the files who is simply Mr. Smith.
The essay it would have made

Concealment as the spine: twenty million dollars to MIT as Mr. Smith, a secret held eight years; a biography he edited himself; ashes under a name and two dates; a company that curated its own archive.

Escape Velocity

The image escapes every body that holds it, and each escape kills its keeper.

4What became of it The frame survived at the edges: the essay's why-now is that the arc from 1888 is complete, and the closing looks at what a photograph is once it needs no camera.
The essay it would have made

The image as the protagonist and the humans as its keepers: glass, silver, acetate, silicon, and now a picture with no lens, no light and no moment.

The Company Town

A man built a city to hold his company, and when the company died the city had nothing left to be.

5What became of it Became the method and the giving paragraphs: one place done completely, and the gifts that outlived the company that paid for them.
The essay it would have made

Rochester as the through-line character: the medical school, the dental dispensary, the music school, the city-manager campaign, sixty thousand jobs at the peak and thirteen hundred now.

The Last Religion of the Burned-Over District

The region that mass-produced new beliefs produced one last one: a machine for believing your own eyes.

6What became of it Did not carry into the essay; kept for the book.
The essay it would have made

Opens on the burned-over district, a new scripture, a new afterlife and a new abolition within sixty miles, and ends with an atheist inventing the district's most successful faith, the snapshot.

Douglass's Promise

Douglass argued in Rochester that the camera would make every person undeniable; Eastman built the machine and balanced it for white skin.

7What became of it Cut, twice, at Evan's direction: without Douglass in the essay the method could not be shown. Kept for its own essay.
The essay it would have made

Two men in one city who never meet, the promise industrialised, and the chemistry that betrayed it, running from the Shirley card into firmware and model weights.

One City, Three Networks

Every network that carries the world's information is built by people who cannot conceive of the one that replaces it, and Rochester built three.

8What became of it Did not carry into the essay; kept in the book as the supply-chain edge.
The essay it would have made

The canal, the telegraph and the image as freight, each obsoleting the last from inside the same city, with Xerox as the fourth.

The Dead Hand

A man can bequeath instructions but not the judgment that wrote them.

9What became of it The instructions and the note survived: the seven words, and the company running on his orders for another eighty years.
The essay it would have made

A book made of documents: the 1904 policy to cover every avenue, the 1914 judgment to forget Goodwin, the will signed ninety minutes before the shot, and the 1973 memo that repeated the sentence with its wisdom removed.

The Habit

He invented a business that made everyone dependent, and the last addict was his own company.

10What became of it Present in the essay as the promise on the box and as the reason the incumbent story is incomplete.
The essay it would have made

The camera cheap, the film forever, the developing back to Rochester; then a company hooked on its own margins, refusing digital rationally right up until it was fatal.
